# Context Core

What this business is aiming at, and for whom. Everything the system reads
before it drafts, prices or answers in the business's name.

Most of it is empty today. That is deliberate. A pack full of guesses about
your business would cost you weeks in corrections. Each folder says what goes
in it; fill the ones that matter first.

| Folder | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| `strategic-identity/` | Where the business is heading. Seeded from your Self as Code, by your routing decision |
| `business/` | What the business is, how it sells, what it does not do |
| `operations/` | How the work gets done: the method, the lifecycle, the judgement calls |
| `organisation/` | The team, who does what, how decisions get made |
| `value-offers/` | What the business sells and how it is priced |
| `customer-intelligence/` | Who you serve, what they ask, what they say |
| `market-intelligence/` | The market you sell into |
| `competitive-intelligence/` | Who else is in it, where you win and lose |
| `messaging/` | How the business talks to customers, and the replies that work |
| `voice/` | How the business sounds, and the rules that keep it |
| `correspondence/` | Emails sent, saved as they go |
