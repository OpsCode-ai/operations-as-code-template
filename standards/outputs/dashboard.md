# A dashboard, drawn for this business

Reads first: `vision.md`, `standards/brand.md`, then `context-core/voice/`
for every word on it.

- One page. The answer at the top: how the business is going, in one line.
  Then what it runs on, what still runs by hand, where each goal stands, and
  what moved this week.
- The business's ink, paper, one accent and typeface. Nothing else. Plain
  black on white if none are chosen.
- Numbers in small tables or on their own line, each saying where it came
  from: the register, a goal's row, a log. No gauges, no dials, no number
  made up to fill a tile.
- Space, not boxes. No panel inside a panel. No code font for ordinary
  words. It is a page made for the people in this business, not the page
  every AI tool makes.
- Checked before it is shown: the colours on the page are the ones in
  `brand.md` (`python3 scripts/oac-vision.py check <page>`), and the words
  pass the same two checks as an email.
