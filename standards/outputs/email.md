# An email, in the business's name

Reads first: `context-core/voice/`, then `standards/brand.md` if the email
carries a signature.

- It sounds like the business. The rules in `context-core/voice/` are the
  measure.
- Short sentences. Plain words. One ask per email. Australian English.
- No line a machine would write: no 'I hope this finds you well', no 'please
  don't hesitate', no three things for the rhythm of it, no dashes for pauses.
- Checked before it is shown: the reading grade and the signs of machine
  writing. Shown to a person before it is sent, always. Saved to
  `context-core/correspondence/` as it goes.
