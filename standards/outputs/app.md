# An app, built for this business

Reads first: `vision.md`, `standards/brand.md`, `context-core/voice/`, and
this page.

- Every screen follows `outputs/page.md`. Every word a person sees follows
  `outputs/email.md`.
- Nothing nobody asked for: no extra buttons, no settings nobody needs, no
  'coming soon'.
- It runs on the business's own machines first. Nothing leaves them unless
  someone says so.
- Checked before it is shown: the colours are the business's, the words pass
  the two checks, and it does the one thing it was asked for.
