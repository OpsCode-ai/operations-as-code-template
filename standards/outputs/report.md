# A report, for this business

Reads first: `vision.md`, `context-core/voice/` and `standards/brand.md`.

- The answer first, then the evidence. A number sits in a table, not a
  sentence.
- Every number says where it came from: the register, a goal's row, a log
  entry, a commit. Anything that cannot be grounded is marked, never guessed.
- The letterhead at the top: the two lines in `brand.md`, in the business's
  typeface, no logo. Its colours if it is a page; plain black on white if
  none are chosen.
- No headings that are moods, no bold for emphasis, no summary at the end
  that repeats the top.
- Checked before it is shown: the reading grade, the signs of machine
  writing, and that the colours used are the business's
  (`python3 scripts/oac-vision.py check <page>`).
