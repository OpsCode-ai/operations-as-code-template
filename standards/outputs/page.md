# A page, built for this business

Reads first: `vision.md` and `standards/brand.md`, then `context-core/voice/`
for every word on it.

- The business's colours, its typeface, light or dark as chosen. Nothing
  else.
- The words follow `outputs/email.md`'s rules: the business's, short, plain.
- It works on a phone first. One column. No decoration that says nothing.
- Checked before it is shown: the colours on the page are the ones in
  `brand.md` (`python3 scripts/oac-vision.py check <page>`), and the words
  pass the same two checks as an email.
